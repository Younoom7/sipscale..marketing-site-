Partnership details

What this is

We work with small and growing Instagram pages to turn their reach into real income, with no upfront cost and no extra work on your side. We find paid content campaigns and prepare ready-to-post content, and you post it on your page. When a reel earns, the earnings are shared on terms agreed in writing before you start.

You keep full control of your page. We never ask for your password.

Who this is for

  • Your page is active and you haven’t started earning from sponsorships yet, or want to earn more
  • You post general or viral-style content, not locked to one niche
  • Some of your reels get far more views than your average
  • You can post consistently, at least 3 to 5 reels per week

Requirements for payment

Campaigns have rules, and a reel only earns if they are met. To be paid for a post, you agree to:

  • Use the exact content, caption format and audio provided
  • Include any required hashtags or mentions
  • Post on the agreed schedule and keep the reel live for at least 30 days
  • Not delete, edit or hide the reel before payout
  • Not buy views, followers or engagement
  • Follow the campaign’s country and audience rules

If a post doesn’t meet the campaign requirements and is rejected, it isn’t paid. We always share the requirements before you post.

What we need from you

  • A screenshot of your audience location breakdown (Instagram Insights)
  • A screen recording of your last 30 days of reel views
  • Your preferred payment method
  • Confirmation that you own and control the account

What you can expect from us

  • Clear requirements for every campaign before you post
  • Ready-to-post content, with sometimes a small on-screen text edit expected from you
  • Honest reporting of views, approvals and payouts for each post
  • Your payout within 3 to 5 days of the campaign paying out, with proof
  • No pressure. You can stop at any time.

Be aware of the risks

  • Earnings are not guaranteed. Some reels will earn little or nothing.
  • Campaigns can reject posts or change their rules.
  • Payout amounts depend on where your views come from.

The trial

We start with a 2-week trial of 10 to 15 reels. After that, we review the numbers together and both decide whether to continue.

Simple agreement

Before the trial, both sides confirm the earnings split, the payout timing, that posts failing campaign requirements aren’t paid, and that either side can end the partnership at any time, with approved posts still paid out. The split is shared with you privately in writing.
